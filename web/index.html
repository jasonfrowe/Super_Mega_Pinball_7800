The table

Pop bumpers, slingshots, the M-E-G-A lanes, a wire ramp over the top, drop targets that light the lock, and three-ball multiball. The flippers are real rotating capsules, and the physics runs on the AstroCart's math unit, with music on two POKEY chips.
